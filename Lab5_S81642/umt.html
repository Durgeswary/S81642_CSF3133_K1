About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT), formerly known as Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM), is a public university located in Kuala Nerus District, Terengganu, Malaysia. It was officially chartered as a full-fledged university on 1 February 2007 and specializes in marine science, oceanography, and aquatic resource management.

Originally established as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science, the institution grew into Terengganu University College (KUT) in May 1999 as an associate campus of UPM. After gaining autonomy in May 2001 and being renamed KUSTEM in June 2001, it eventually received full university status and adopted its current name in 2007.

In 2015, Universiti Malaysia Terengganu was granted full autonomy status by the Ministry of Higher Education. Today, UMT continues to lead research in marine and freshwater biology while serving students across multiple specialized faculties.
