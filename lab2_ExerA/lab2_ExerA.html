About the Event

Career Day 2026 is organised by the Faculty of Computer Science and Mathematics to help students explore career opportunities and prepare for their future careers.

Speaker : Software Engineer

A professional software engineer will share experiences about software development, programming skills and career opportunities in the IT industry.

Activity: Resume Writing Workshop

Students will learn how to prepare a professional resume for future job applications.
